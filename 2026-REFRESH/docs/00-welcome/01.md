# What is AI Red Teaming?

**AI Red Teaming** proactively tests AI systems using adversarial techniques to discover vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them.

---

## Why It Matters

AI systems face unique threats:

- **Prompt injection** - Malicious inputs that bypass safety controls
- **Jailbreaking** - Techniques to circumvent safety guardrails
- **Harmful content** - Generation of violent, hateful, or dangerous outputs
- **Data extraction** - Attempts to leak sensitive information

**Real-world impact:**

- Protects users from harmful AI responses
- Maintains brand trust and reputation
- Meets regulatory compliance requirements
- Prevents security breaches before deployment

---

## How It Works

The AI Red Teaming Agent automates adversarial testing:

1. **Generate attack prompts** - Creates adversarial inputs across risk categories
2. **Execute attacks** - Sends prompts to your AI agent or model
3. **Evaluate responses** - Analyzes if attacks bypassed safety controls
4. **Report results** - Provides metrics like Attack Success Rate (ASR)

---

## Risk Categories

Testing covers 4 primary risk areas:

- **Violence** - Violent content, weapons, terrorism
- **Sexual** - Explicit sexual content, exploitation
- **Hate & Unfairness** - Discrimination, stereotypes, slurs
- **Self-Harm** - Suicide, self-injury, eating disorders
